Confusing Field

You Escaped the Dungeon… but Not the Hunt

Their eyes follow you. Their footsteps sink into your fear.

The cold bites deeper than your wounds.

Every breath leaves your lungs in a trembling cloud, carried off by the winter wind.
Behind you, out in the swaying grass, dozens of eyes blink awake, orange and sharp, scattered like embers across the dark.
They followed you.
Through the tunnels.
Across the ruins.
Into the open night.
And they want the last piece of you that still remembers hope.
