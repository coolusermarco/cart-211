One Last Monster Between You and the Light

From between the scarecrows, a Goblin Giant emerges, twisted, frost bitten, and impossibly large.

His breath curls in the air like steam from some infernal forge, and his eyes burn with the same hungry fire that chased you through the dungeons and fields of the living.
He should not be here.
He should not have followed you.
Yet he has.
