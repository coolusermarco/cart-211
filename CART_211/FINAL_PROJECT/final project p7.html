Abandonned Garden

The Mage Has Fallen… and the Horde Hungers

Their shadows multiply faster than breath escapes you.

You were too late.

The goblins tore the mage apart before your eyes, scattering his runes across the soil like broken seeds. Now the garden trembles with their growls more of them forcing their way through shattered panes, crawling from the vines, rising from the very dirt.
Their eyes burn brighter.
Their steps grow louder.
Their numbers swell like rot in a forgotten garden.
There is no refuge left.
No circle of protection.
No time to pray.
Only the swarm…
and the certainty that you will be devoured next.
