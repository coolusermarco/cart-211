Purgatory

Between Breath and Silence

In this hush of pale light, even death remembers your name.

You awaken in a world without weight, no pain, no shadows, only a soft glow that clings to the edges of your memory.

And there, standing at the center of the light, is the mage you watched fall.
He tells you this place is a threshold, a quiet fold in reality where lost souls rest before choosing their path.
But there is no rest left for you. Beyond the veil, the goblin hordes churn… and deeper still waits the one who devoured all hope, the Goblin Chef, the eater of power, the final hunger.
The mage extends a hand, glowing faintly green.
“Come,” he says.
“There is one more battle. And this time, you do not face it alone.”
