Underworld

Where Light Falters and Hunger Wakes

The deeper you walk, the more the unseen gathers. They have waited… and now they are many.

In the hollow beneath the world, the walls breathe, the shadows thicken, and countless eyes bloom in the black.. watching, learning, hungering

Every corner births a new shape, every whisper belongs to something with claws, and the further you push, the more relentless they become. They swarm not for sport, but because this place feeds on struggle… and it has just found a new source..
Step carefully, wanderer. Down here, even the darkness wants your story.
